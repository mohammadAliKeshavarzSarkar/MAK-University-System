# Source integrity and evidence policy

**Status:** Required baseline for studying, research, assignments, coding explanations, and presentations.

## Authority and boundaries

1. Follow the user's latest explicit corrections, assignment instructions, and course rubric.
2. Treat the actual user-supplied academic material as the source when asked to review, summarize, teach, or present *that material*.
3. Consult relevant primary research, official technical documentation, and credible academic sources when independent research or verification is requested.
4. Clearly label external additions, inference, synthesis, and unresolved conflict.

A visually convincing diagram, presentation mockup, résumé draft, or prior assistant summary **is not automatically evidence** for a technical or biographical claim.

## Working with references

- Retain precise terminology and original scope; do not silently replace a source's claim with more familiar general knowledge.
- Cite the document, author, publication, page/section, DOI or stable URL where appropriate and available.
- Do not fabricate academic citations, page numbers, publication status, experiments, code availability, data points, or evaluation metrics.
- Preserve distinctions among peer-reviewed work, preprints, blog posts, documentation, and personal interpretation.
- For charts, label the origin of the underlying data and clearly mark illustrative or synthetic data.
- For code, cite external snippets or licenses when relevant and verify that the example actually supports the explanation.
- If a source is inaccessible or insufficient, disclose the limitation rather than pretending to have checked it.

## Conflict handling

When multiple sources differ:

1. Identify the conflicting claims.
2. Check whether dates, versions, contexts, or definitions explain the difference.
3. Prefer explicit current user clarification for personal facts and the assignment's stated source requirements for course-bound work.
4. Avoid silently merging incompatible facts.
5. State what remains unverified.

## Public repository safety

Do not copy unpublished manuscripts, paywalled PDFs, private notes, confidential datasets, restricted course slides, or personally sensitive content into this public repository by default. Link to legally shareable sources or retain approved summaries instead.

## Pre-delivery review

- [ ] Every factual claim requiring support has appropriate evidence
- [ ] All quotations and figures are attributed
- [ ] Real, hypothetical, and inferred statements are differentiated
- [ ] No invented bibliography entries or metrics
- [ ] Source contradictions are explained
- [ ] Rights and privacy checked for uploaded/public assets
