# FRAME / IMAGE — Reference catalog

These are the references used or identified in the twelve guided lessons. This consolidation does not independently revalidate every web page or license. Reopen the original source when making a source-specific claim, verify current tool/version behavior, and check individual asset rights at production time. FRAME presentation adaptations are not requirements imposed by the sources.

## Visual literacy, research and art direction

- [Mayer — Coherence Principle](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108894333.019): exclude irrelevant instructional material; consult accessible chapter text before claiming specific research results.
- [Assertion–Evidence Approach](https://www.assertion-evidence.org/): technical messages supported by visual evidence.
- [W3C Images Tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/), [Complex Images](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/): image function and text alternatives; originally web guidance.
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/) and [license](https://unsplash.com/license); [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) and [reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia); [Openverse](https://openverse.org/); [NASA Image Library](https://images.nasa.gov/): discovery and authentic imagery where appropriate. Aggregators and image search do not grant rights; check original file pages, usage terms and third-party rights.
- Adobe [minimalist photography](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/type/minimalist-photography.html), [high contrast](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/high-contrast.html), [composition](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/composition.html), [rule of thirds](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/photography/technique/rule-of-thirds.html), [tone and color](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom-classic/desktop/process-and-develop-photos/image-tone-color.html): photographic technique references.
- [Poster House — Swiss Grid](https://swissgrid.posterhouse.org/); [Carbon — Typography](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/typography/overview): reuse existing [design-learning references](../design-learning/SOURCES.md).
- W3C [Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html) and [Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum): web accessibility screening; not a substitute for slide projection testing.

## Creation, illustration and integrity

- [OpenAI image prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-prompting), [ChatGPT image generation learning reference](https://openai.com/academy/image-generation/), [Adobe Firefly prompting](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/work-with-images/generate-images/writing-effective-text-prompts.html): prompt structure and refinement; check available capabilities rather than assume a model, mask or output ratio.
- [Adobe Generative Fill](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/desktop/create-open-import-images/create-images/edit-images-with-generative-fill.html): selective editing workflow; not proof of exact preservation.
- [C4 model](https://c4model.com/) and [diagram review checklist](https://c4model.com/diagrams/checklist): architecture abstraction, scope and relations. [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/), [PlantUML](https://plantuml.com/), [Graphviz](https://graphviz.org/), [diagrams.net](https://www.drawio.com/): diagram authoring choices, not mandatory dependencies.
- [Nielsen Norman Group — Mental Models](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mental-models/): conceptual background for relating familiar and unfamiliar systems; not evidence that a specific FRAME metaphor improves comprehension.
- [Nature — image communication guidelines](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-023-01987-9), [image integrity guide](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/image-integrity/): consult applicable research/disciplinary policies before altering evidence.

## Format, attribution and delivery

- W3C [SVG](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG/) and [PNG](https://www.w3.org/TR/png/) specifications.
- Microsoft [crop pictures](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/graphics-visuals/crop-a-picture-in-office), [edit SVG](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/graphics-visuals/edit-svg-images-in-microsoft-365), [image resolution](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/graphics-visuals/change-the-default-resolution-for-inserting-pictures-in-office), [file-size reduction](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/powerpoint/reduce-the-file-size-of-your-powerpoint-presentations), [accessible presentations](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/powerpoint/make-your-powerpoint-presentations-accessible-to-people-with-disabilities): check actual Office version and operating system.
- Creative Commons [license types](https://creativecommons.org/cc-licenses/) and [reuse/attribution](https://creativecommons.org/reusing-cc-licensed-content/): review the specific license text and intended use, attribution and modifications. Other rights can remain; CC0 is a public-domain dedication tool.
