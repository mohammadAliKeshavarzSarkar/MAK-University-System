# FRAME Design Learning — Source Catalog and Evidence Register

**Maintained:** 2026-11-23. These are recommended study references, **not** a replacement for course materials or approved MAK design standards. All seven URLs were reopened on 2026-11-23.

| Reference | What the source actually addresses | Module(s) | Access / limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Poster House — The Swiss Grid](https://swissgrid.posterhouse.org/) | History and pedagogy of the Swiss Grid, poster examples, flexible grid use, critiques and alterations | Grid, hierarchy | Public exhibition microsite. Its historical claims and poster context should not be mistaken for PowerPoint specifications. |
| [Niggli — Grid Systems in Graphic Design](https://niggli.ch/en/products/rastersysteme-fur-die-visuelle-gestaltung) | Josef Müller-Brockmann's grid-design handbook, grids with 8–32 fields and applications across print/exhibition settings | Grid, typography, hierarchy, rhythm | **Publisher catalog/description and preview, not the full 176-page book.** Do not claim detailed chapter/page prescriptions without inspecting supplied pages. |
| [IBM Carbon — Typography](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/typography/overview) | IBM Plex-based type tokens, scale, productive vs expressive sets, style and color | Typography, hierarchy | UI system. Learn type-scale reasoning, not IBM Plex as the MAK brand font or Carbon pixel values as classroom standards. |
| [Material Design 1 — Choreography](https://m1.material.io/motion/choreography.html) | Transition continuity, shared focal elements, creating surfaces, coordinated focus | Motion | **Legacy Material Design 1 UI** guidance, not PowerPoint animation settings. |
| [Atlassian Design — Motion](https://atlassian.design/foundations/motion) | Intentional motion, accessible reduced-motion choices, duration/easing/property tokens | Motion | UI duration ranges apply to interactions and transitions, not automatically to spoken lecture pacing. Site currently describes a motion early-access rollout. |
| [Adobe Spectrum 2 — Motion](https://spectrum.adobe.com/foundations/behavior/motion) | Purposeful motion, linear/diagonal/clockwise direction and RTL consideration | Motion | Current page says Spectrum 2 motion guidance is still under development. Avoid presenting it as a complete standard. |
| [IBM Carbon — Motion](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/motion/overview) | Productive vs expressive UI motion, easing, duration and orchestration | Motion, rhythm | UI system; any slide sequencing or motion prescriptions are FRAME adaptations. |
